How prices are written

Coins are drawn at their real sizes compared with each other. Levels 1 to 4 use prices under €1 or whole euros. Level 5 mixes euros and cents. Level 6 takes some coins out of the purse, so your child has to find another way to pay. Turn the total off when they’re ready to add up in their head.
