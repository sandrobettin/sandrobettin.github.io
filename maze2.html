Maze Book

Draw a path through each maze. Some mazes ask you to collect things in the right order.

The dots on each card are its levels, and they fill in as you finish them: green is easy, yellow is medium, red is difficult. Every maze is new each time you play.

For grown-ups

Sound effects
Read instructions aloud
Arrow buttons under the maze
Crayon line behind the player
Number mazes go up to

Children can drag the character with a finger, use the arrow keys, or turn on the arrow buttons. The 💡 button shows what to do next. In the dressing mazes, clothes only go on in an order that makes sense: underpants before trousers or tights, tights before the skirt, the undershirt before the T-shirt, the T-shirt before the sweater, the sweater before the hat, and socks, tights and trousers before shoes. Anything else can go on in any order.
